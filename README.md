# Rollover

**The checklist that never forgets.**

A simple, powerful daily task manager designed specifically for bookkeepers, admins, and professionals who juggle recurring tasks with unpredictable to-dos.

[**→ Use It Now**](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sallybrooke/rollover/main/rollover.html) | Download and open in your browser

-----

## Why This Exists

Most task apps force you to choose:

- ❌ Rigid habit trackers (can’t handle ad-hoc tasks)
- ❌ Complex project managers (overkill for daily routines)
- ❌ Paper checklists (no rollover, manual rewriting every day)

**Daily Checklist gives you the best of both worlds:**

- ✅ Recurring tasks auto-generate daily
- ✅ One-off tasks roll over until completed
- ✅ Print-friendly (works like your handwritten lists)
- ✅ Completely private (data stays in your browser)

## How It Works

### Three Types of Tasks:

**⭐ Static Tasks** - Every single day

- Morning email check
- Daily reconciliation
- End-of-day review

**📅 Day-Specific Tasks** - Certain days of the week

- Monday: Weekly reports
- Thursday: Payroll prep
- Friday: Accounts payable

**📆 Monthly Tasks** - Specific dates each month

- 1st: Monthly billing
- 15th: Sales tax filing
- 30th: Month-end close

**Weekend tasks automatically appear on Friday** (or roll to Monday if you don’t finish them).

### The Daily Workflow:

**Morning (2 minutes):**

1. Open the app
1. Print today’s checklist
1. Work from paper all day

**End of Day (3 minutes):**

1. Check off what you completed
1. Add any handwritten notes from the printout
1. Tomorrow’s list auto-generates with rollovers

Nothing falls through the cracks.

## Quick Start

### 1. Download & Open

- Download `rollover.html`
- Save it somewhere permanent (Desktop or Documents)
- Double-click to open in your browser
- Bookmark it for daily access

### 2. Set Up Your Tasks (One-Time)

- Click “Setup Tasks” tab
- Add your recurring tasks in three sections
- Click “Save & Refresh”
- Done!

### 3. Use It Daily

- Open the file each morning
- Your checklist is ready
- Print it or use it digitally
- Check off tasks as you go

**That’s it.** No account, no subscription, no complexity.

## FAQ

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: In your browser’s localStorage, tied to this specific HTML file. As long as you open the same file in the same browser, your data persists.

**Q: Can I use it on multiple devices?**  
A: Not yet - this version is local-only. Cloud sync is planned for a future paid version.

**Q: What if I lose my data?**  
A: Your browser stores it locally. If you clear your browser data or delete the file, tasks are gone. Pro tip: Keep the HTML file backed up.

**Q: Can I customize the look?**  
A: Not in the UI, but you can edit the HTML/CSS if you’re comfortable with code.

**Q: Does it work offline?**  
A: Yes! After the first load (which downloads fonts), it works completely offline.

**Q: Can I see the code?**  
A: Absolutely. It’s a single HTML file - right-click and “View Source” to see everything.

## Technical Details
